Pompompurin of Love

Participant 3, age 17 · Companionship

With a heart-shaped wand in hand and a red beret neatly glued in between his ears, Pompompurin winks to establish his authority on spreading love and peace. At night, Pompompurin of Love is always ready to be cuddled and squeezed and drooled on, making sure his owner falls into a deep, relaxing sleep. During the day, though, Pompompurin naturally takes the role of a babied figure. Until today, his owner still dotes on him like an older sibling to their youngest.
