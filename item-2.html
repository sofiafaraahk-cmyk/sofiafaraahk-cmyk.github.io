Mintchhichi

Participant 2, age 18 · Companionship

As the firstborn of seven monchhichi siblings, Mintchhichi had always held a special place in its owner’s heart. Not only does its color align with the owner’s preferences, Mintchhichi’s arrival during a difficult period gave it a deeper role as a source of comfort and support. A guiding light who paved the way out of a dark time, a firstborn, and a beloved doll; Mintchhichi will always be a symbol of care and affection for its owner.
