Hachimi Seal "Wildaughter" Sakura

Participant 1, age 17 · Companionship

Fat, pink, and cute! Wildaughter was a gift from a close friend, initially given to replace its lost predecessor, yet soon established its own personal tie with its owner. Being irresistibly squishy and gentle to the touch, Hachimi Seal Sakura reminded its owner of themself. The strong sense of attachment shared between Wildaughter and its owner is evidence of certain objects taking the role of intimate companion.
