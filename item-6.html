Goalkeeper's Glove

Participant 9, age 15 · Memory

An everyday “battle equipment” on the football field. The goalkeeper glove is utilized by the owner not only though its usage on the field, rather it’s used to also carry the memories of friendship, achievement, and hardship throughout the years. To the owner, this particular pair of gloves hold memories that cannot simply be replaced by a pair of new ones.
