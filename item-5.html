Cutie Pie

Participant 5, grade 10 · Memory

A giant cat plushie gifted by the owner’s uncle in their childhood. Cutie Pie, almost named Cutie Meow-Meow, had travelled with its owner across cities and countries as a constant companion. Having survived a long journey, the plushie now carries memories of the owner’s younger self and the presence of a missed family.
