Airpods

Participant 1, age 17 · Effort and Result

Obtained after a scuba-diving mishap in Bali that resulted in the loss of a previous pair, these airpods take more than the role of daily technology. In difficult situations, it proves useful to be the source of the owner’s comfort. The presence of these airpods also brings forth memories of family, as well as the owner’s process from repeatedly losing everyday objects to finally taking better care of them.
