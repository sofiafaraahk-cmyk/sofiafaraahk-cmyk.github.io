RusPrus Small Plushies

Participant 7, age 18 · Self and Surrounding

These small, chibi plushies of Russia and Prussia were found abruptly during its owner’s family trip overseas. In particular, Prussia had long since become a character in which its owner has a deep personal connection with, even described as a son they had cared for and carried through their everyday life. The owner’s affection towards these plushies has become a very apparent form of identity recognized by their peers.
