Lee Taeyong Photocard

Participant 6, age 17 · Effort and Result

An item that had been on the owner’s wishlist for a while which quickly became their treasured find after picking it up at a small stall in a local shopping mall. Over time, the photocard has become so closely associated with its owner that their friends recognize it as hers, which reflects the photocard and Taeyong’s role as a visible part of the owner’s identity.
